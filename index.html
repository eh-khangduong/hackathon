Key Feature Architecture

A complete ecosystem to map and bridge capability gaps.

Dynamic Career Blueprinting

  • Target Role Mapping Select desired roles (e.g., Data Analyst to Product Manager) to set your North Star.
  • Automated Gap Analysis AI generates a personalized Skill Gap Report based on current competencies vs requirements.
  • Visual Career Trees Interactive maps showing trajectories, lateral moves, and high-growth tracks.

Up-skilling & Training Hub

  • Micro-Learning Integration Seamless connection to internal L&D, Coursera, or LinkedIn Learning for missing competencies.
  • AI Learning Assistants AI tutors guide candidates through complex topics and simulate tailored interview scenarios.
  • Certifications & Badges Completed modules automatically yield verified badges that update your platform profile.

"Gig & Work" Marketplace

  • Micro-Internships Short-term (5-20hr) projects to apply new skills in live internal or external environments.
  • Shadowing Programs Get paired with mentors in your target role for 1–2 days of direct job-shadowing.
  • Pro-Bono / Volunteer Gain verifiable hands-on experience through non-profit or open-source partnerships.

Opportunity Matching

  • Readiness Score Employers view a score based on past experience, recent training, and platform project performance.
  • Blind Skill-Based Matching Opt to remain anonymous to recruiters until matched purely on skill relevance to reduce bias.
  • Pre-Vetted Talent Employers receive candidates with proven drive and exact skill matches, reducing time-to-hire.
